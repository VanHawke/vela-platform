＋Binding addendum — standard drafter
The step editor in render 1 must be built on the platform standard drafter, src/components/kiko/EmailDraft.jsx — the same frame used in Kiko chat, contact detail and the dashboard: tone CTAs, inline edit, copy, scheduled send, and team Gmail send with the Sunny / Matt sender identity. Campaign context (channel, delay, step position) wraps the frame; it does not replace it. Test-send (to me / to a chosen recipient, email and LinkedIn) is folded into the standard frame as a first-class action so one drafter serves the whole platform. The wizard step 2 textareas migrate to the same frame in the same build. No bespoke composers anywhere after this ship.
