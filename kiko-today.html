Common questions

Everything you'd want to know.

How does Kiko know which items to prioritise?+
Kiko ranks items by predicted impact on pipeline value — combining propensity scores, signal recency, sequence stage, and your historical conversion patterns. Replies from interested prospects always surface above outbound drafts. Urgent signals (renewals, exec moves, brand budget filings) elevate to the top.
What happens when I skip an item?+
Skipped items aren't deleted — they're snoozed for 24 hours and re-evaluated. If the signal degrades or the prospect goes cold, Kiko removes it from the queue. If it stays relevant, it returns the next morning with updated context.
Can I edit Kiko's drafts before sending?+
Always. Hit E (or click Edit) to open the full draft in an editor with the original research and source signal attached. Every edit you make trains Kiko's tone model. By week three the drafts converge on your voice — most users approve 90%+ without any changes.
What if I'm travelling and miss a morning?+
Outbound sequences pause until you're back. Inbound replies still get classified and drafted, ready for your approval whenever you open the queue. Urgent signals (replies marked as interested, meeting requests, urgent renewals) trigger a notification rather than waiting.
How is Today different from a normal email inbox?+
Three things. First, every item arrives pre-researched with full prospect context. Second, drafts and responses are written, not just suggested. Third, the queue is collapsed across email + LinkedIn + signals into one workflow — you don't switch tools, you switch decisions.

Common questions

Things people ask before going live.

What does Kiko do overnight, exactly? +
Pulls fresh signals across 75 sources, scores prospects against your campaigns, drafts every queued touch personalised to the latest data on each prospect, classifies overnight replies by intent, and prioritises tomorrow's queue by what moves your pipeline most.
Can I edit Kiko's drafts before sending? +
Yes — every draft is editable inline. Common edits get learned and reflected in future drafts. You can also tell Kiko in plain English how to revise: "tighten by 30%", "make it more direct", "reference the FT piece".
Does Kiko send anything without my approval? +
Never. Every email and LinkedIn message requires a single tap of approval. Auto-pause kicks in if Kiko detects risk — e.g. an out-of-office reply, a do-not-contact request, or a stage gate trigger.
What happens if I'm away for a week? +
Kiko continues monitoring signals and queueing drafts. Nothing sends without you. When you return, the queue prioritises what's most time-sensitive — meeting requests, hot replies, and signals with decay risk.
